The pitch

He's blind.
You're the passenger.

A horse in opaque goggles just took the wheel. The city is a blur. Your only job? Stay in the cab long enough to laugh — or scream — your way to the destination.

Blind Taxi is a chaotic comedy driving experience from Bihter Games: high speed, bad decisions, and the world's most confident blindfolded chauffeur.
